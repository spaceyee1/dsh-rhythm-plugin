Package provenance
- src/: implementation written for this project with AI assistance.
- desktop/: project-specific XAML, PowerShell and embedded C# implementation.

  oscillators in the embedded C# helper; no external audio samples are bundled.
- src/core.mjs: original continuous difficulty and tap/chord/hold chart engine.
- src/question-bank.mjs: newly authored questions and parameterized exercises
